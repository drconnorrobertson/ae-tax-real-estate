Focused implementation guides

Resolve the related evidence question before carrying a planning assumption into implementation.

  • Rental closing cost allocation: A closing statement combines financing, acquisition and prepaid operating items.
  • Renovation contractor draw reconciliation: A contractor bills progress draws rather than individual installed items.
  • Roof project repair review: A roof project combines small repairs, replacement and related damage work.
  • HVAC replacement documentation: A rental replaces equipment that serves more than one unit or activity.
  • Appliance replacement asset history: A rental replaces refrigerators or other separately identified items.
  • Furniture inventory at rental turnover: Furniture changes as guests, owners and cleaners report loss or damage.
  • Security deposit accounting: A landlord holds tenant deposits and sometimes applies them to damage or unpaid rent.
  • Property manager statement reconciliation: A manager collects rent and pays expenses before transferring cash to the owner.
  • Rental insurance claim restoration: Insurance proceeds and repair invoices arrive at different times after property damage.
  • Rental property tax escrow reconciliation: Mortgage payments include escrow deposits that differ from actual tax payments.
  • Rental placed-in-service evidence: A rental is purchased, repaired, listed and first occupied on different dates.
  • Rental conversion use records: A property moves from personal use into a rental activity.
  • Vacation rental owner stays: Guests and the owner use the same property during the year.
  • STR guest service evidence: A short-term rental provides services that vary across bookings.
  • Rental participation task logs: An owner wants a review of involvement in operating a rental.
  • Real estate professional work calendar: A taxpayer performs work across rentals, a separate profession and other activities.
  • Rental loan proceeds use tracing: An owner refinances and distributes some proceeds while using other funds for improvements.
  • Rental sale asset register: A rental sale includes land, building and separately tracked assets.
  • Rental portfolio shared expenses: An owner pays bills covering multiple properties and personal activity.
  • Prior rental depreciation gaps: Old returns and available depreciation schedules do not agree.
