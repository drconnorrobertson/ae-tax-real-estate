Self-storage properties: Depreciation history request

Recover prior schedules and reporting before changing the property analysis. Map storage buildings, access systems, paved areas and later additions.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Collect filed returns, asset exports, prior reports and later additions. Identify missing records and who has requested them. Do not assume no depreciation was reported merely because the owner cannot find the schedule.

Map storage buildings, access systems, paved areas and later additions.

A situation to work through

An owner expands units and upgrades a gate in different phases.

For this situation, start with site plan. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Expected handoff

A year-by-year history and missing-record log.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
