Restaurant buildings: Placed-in-service evidence

Build the factual timeline of readiness for intended use. Document kitchen equipment, restaurant installations and building ownership.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Distinguish purchase, delivery, installation, completion and operational readiness. Keep property-level and component-level records separate when projects are phased. The worksheet records evidence and does not determine the final depreciation start date.

Document kitchen equipment, restaurant installations and building ownership.

A situation to work through

A restaurant owns kitchen equipment in a leased building.

For this situation, start with equipment inventory. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Do not assume all visible components belong to the property owner.

One payment date is used for every component.

Question for the reviewer

What records establish readiness for each relevant asset or phase?

Expected handoff

A dated readiness timeline for preparer review.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
