Manufacturing properties: Acquisition basis bridge

Connect the closing record to the property cost pool supplied for review. Describe machinery-related work and general plant construction separately.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Record the source purchase figures, separately identified items, land-allocation support and adjustments determined by the preparer. Reconcile to the current schedule instead of adding replacement prices as new acquisition spending.

Describe machinery-related work and general plant construction separately.

A situation to work through

A new production line uses infrastructure shared with existing operations.

For this situation, start with machine layout. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Record what the installation serves and which construction remained.

Purchase and schedule totals do not reconcile.

Question for the reviewer

Which source figures and allocation decisions form the cost pool being analyzed?

Expected handoff

A closing-to-schedule bridge with unresolved differences.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
