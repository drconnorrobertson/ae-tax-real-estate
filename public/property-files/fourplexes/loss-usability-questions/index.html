Fourplexes: Loss usability questions

Connect the property estimate to the owner-level review. Reconcile apartment, common-area and exterior work to the total project.

Preparation resource. Record completion does not determine eligibility, deductions or tax savings. Ask your qualified tax professional to resolve the facts and applicable rules.

What this file should establish

Prepare income categories, ownership, activity facts and existing suspended amounts. Ask the preparer which limitations apply and in what order. A large depreciation estimate is not a guarantee of an immediately usable loss.

Reconcile apartment, common-area and exterior work to the total project.

A situation to work through

A fourplex renovation combines roofs, landscaping and unit fixtures.

For this situation, start with unit diagrams. Identify the period and legal owner before combining it with the other records. Keep the original source and explain any adjustments in a separate reconciliation.

Review point

Do not assign a bundled invoice entirely to one cost category.

The estimate is described as a current tax saving without an owner-level analysis.

Question for the reviewer

Which limitations or missing facts prevent a conclusion about current usability?

Expected handoff

A preparer question list with supporting records.

Separate facts supported by documents from estimates and unresolved assumptions. Assign each missing item to a responsible person and a date. Send the reconciliation alongside the source references so the reviewer can trace each conclusion.
