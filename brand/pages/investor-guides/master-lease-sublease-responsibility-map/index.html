Map master-lease and sublease responsibilities before reviewing cash

Organize the agreements, parties, payments and service obligations in a multi-party rental arrangement.

AE Tax Advisors · Published November 20, 2026

Property operating guides

A property can involve an owner, a master tenant, a subtenant and several service providers. Money passing between these parties does not explain their obligations on its own. A responsibility map helps the owner and advisers review the actual arrangement without merging different agreements. It does not decide whether a proposed use or sublease is permitted.

Identify parties and documents

Record the property, legal parties and relevant agreements, including amendments and consents that have been supplied. Keep the documents in an authorized repository. Use distinct labels for owner, tenant, operator and other roles rather than treating different entities controlled by one person as interchangeable.

Separate payment streams

List who is expected to pay whom, the stated purpose, timing and supporting provision. Distinguish rent from reimbursements, service charges and other transfers. A payment reaching the property manager may contain several streams that require separate explanation. Ask the accountant to determine their treatment rather than using the bank description as the final classification.

Map operating responsibilities

Record the party identified for maintenance, utilities, access, records and other agreed work. Mark unclear responsibilities for qualified review. For a fictional repair request routed through three parties, the map should identify the authorized decision-maker and payer. It should not create authority or obligations that the agreements do not provide.

Compare the map with actual activity

Match statements, invoices, approvals and receipts to the expected arrangement. Identify exceptions such as a party paying another’s bill or retaining money before remittance. Keep the full amount and explanation visible. Do not force actual transactions into a preferred model without resolving why the parties operated differently.

Keep approvals and changes traceable

Record who reviewed uncertain provisions and any confirmed changes to the arrangement. Preserve prior versions with effective dates. Give the complete document map and transaction explanation to legal, tax and accounting advisers as appropriate. A diagram is a navigation aid to the evidence, not a substitute for professional conclusions.

Prepare the review file

  • Parties and agreement versions
  • Separate payment-stream schedule
  • Operating responsibility map
  • Actual transaction reconciliation
  • Consents, amendments and review conclusions
