Map rental vendor access and spending authority before an urgent repair

Define property access, approval limits, communications and completion evidence for recurring and emergency vendors.

AE Tax Advisors · Published November 20, 2026

Property operating guides

A vendor may need to enter a property and perform work while the owner is away. Access to the building and authority to spend are separate permissions. Document both before relying on an urgent repair arrangement. This guide is an operational planning tool; it does not override tenant rights, safety requirements, insurance terms or the applicable contract.

Define the authorized roles

Record the owner contact, manager, backup approver and vendor for each service. Specify who may request work, grant access and approve additional cost. A person who can open the door may not be authorized to approve an expanded repair. Keep the process usable when the primary owner cannot respond.

Set the property access process

Inventory access methods and decide how temporary permissions are issued, checked and removed. Use approved systems and avoid placing reusable credentials in a general vendor spreadsheet. Confirm required coordination with occupants and the responsible manager. Technical security and legal access questions should be reviewed by the appropriate people for the property.

Describe cost approvals and exceptions

State how a vendor obtains approval and documents an estimate or change. For a fictional callout authorized up to $250 for diagnosis, a proposed $1,200 replacement is a separate approval question unless the actual agreement says otherwise. The numbers illustrate process boundaries, not recommended limits or permission to delay necessary safety action.

Specify communication and completion evidence

Ask for the work-order reference, arrival and completion updates, description of work and relevant photographs or reports. Identify where invoices and warranties should be delivered. Distinguish a message saying fixed from evidence of the actual work performed. Let qualified providers define technical testing appropriate to their work.

Review and revoke permissions

After the assignment, reconcile the invoice to approvals and remove access that is no longer needed. Review contacts and recurring permissions periodically, especially when a manager or vendor changes. Keep incident questions and unresolved work visible. Good documentation supports coordination but does not certify the vendor’s credentials or establish that every legal requirement was satisfied.

Prepare the review file

  • Role and backup-approver map
  • Controlled access and occupant coordination
  • Written cost-approval process
  • Work-order and completion evidence
  • Invoice review and permission removal
