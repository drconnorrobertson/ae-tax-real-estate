Plan a rental property manager handoff with balances and records

Prepare a transition file covering tenant ledgers, deposits, vendor commitments, access and unresolved items.

AE Tax Advisors · Published November 20, 2026

Property operating guides

Changing a property manager is a transfer of responsibilities and records, not simply a change in the bank account receiving rent. An owner needs a cutoff, a clear list of deliverables and a way to reconcile what moves between managers. Use this handoff file alongside the agreements and qualified advice relevant to the property; it does not establish either manager’s legal obligations.

Set the cutoff and responsibility map

Record the property list, transition date and person responsible for rent, repairs, notices and owner reporting on either side. Identify work that crosses the cutoff. Confirm arrangements with both managers rather than assuming that changing a portal immediately transfers every responsibility or cancels an existing commitment.

Reconcile financial balances

Request tenant ledgers, owner statements, outstanding bills, held funds and supporting reconciliations for the same date. Keep different types of balances separate. Do not treat a deposit or restricted amount as available operating cash merely because it appears in a transfer. Have the appropriate adviser confirm required handling and any unsettled difference.

Transfer open work and commitments

List repair orders, supplier agreements, insurance matters and tenant issues with status, approval history and next action. For a fictional roof repair approved before transition but billed afterward, retain the approval, work evidence and invoice routing. Otherwise both managers may assume the other is handling it, or the owner may approve the same work twice.

Plan information and access handoffs

Inventory keys, access credentials, approved contacts, vendor histories and system exports. Use authorized channels and remove access according to the agreed process. Check that the incoming team can actually retrieve required records. A list saying documents sent is weaker evidence than a tested export with a recipient and date.

Confirm completion and exceptions

Compare requested deliverables with what arrived, reconcile the transfer and assign unresolved items. Keep the outgoing manager’s final statement and the incoming opening record together. Ask the accountant how to bridge the reporting periods without omissions or double entries. Continue tracking exceptions after the operational transition date until they are resolved.

Prepare the review file

  • Cutoff and responsibility map
  • Financial statements and held-fund records
  • Open repair and supplier commitments
  • Authorized access and export inventory
  • Signed-off exceptions and opening balances

Sources and review scope

These are editorial preparation steps. IRS guidance explains the importance of supporting records and distinguishes types of rental receipts and costs.
