Rental project reserve contribution calculator

Explore rental project reserve contribution calculator using your own inputs, a transparent calculation and a worked example.

AE Tax Advisors · Published November 20, 2026

Rental decision calculators

A project reserve worksheet connects a stated future cash target with funds already set aside and the number of months available to contribute. This calculator divides the remaining funding gap evenly across that period. It provides a planning amount for the specified project, without estimating the project price or deciding whether the reserve is adequate for other property needs.

The starting values are fictional examples. Enter matching periods and comparable costs for your scenario.

Use a defined cash target

Enter the project cash target, the amount already assigned to it and the remaining contribution months. The month count must be greater than zero. A contractor estimate, an internal placeholder and a signed price are different kinds of evidence; label the target accordingly. Funds counted as already saved should be available for this project rather than simultaneously committed to another use. The arithmetic does not verify bank balances or account restrictions.

Understand a zero gap

A $12,000 target with $3,000 saved leaves $9,000 to fund. Dividing that gap across 18 months produces $500 per month. If the amount saved exceeds the target, the required contribution is shown as zero and the excess is reported separately. The calculator does not produce a negative contribution or imply that the excess is available for distribution. Other commitments may still exist outside this worksheet.

Reconcile the schedule with reality

Contributions are assumed to be equal and available by the target date. Interest, inflation, project-cost changes and irregular funding dates are excluded. A project requiring deposits or progress payments before completion may need several earlier funding milestones. Record those milestones separately rather than assuming the final completion date governs every payment. Updating a quote can change the contribution amount even when the number of months stays the same. A reserve allocation by itself does not establish a tax deduction or classify the eventual expenditure.

Worked example

$9,000 remaining over18 months =$500 per month.

What this model leaves out

Interest, inflation and project-cost changes; reserves do not establish a deduction.

Before using the result

  • Name the project and target date on the saved worksheet.
  • Identify the source and date of the cost estimate.
  • Check that saved funds are not counted twice.
  • List deposits and progress payments occurring before completion.
  • Recalculate when price, available funds or timing changes.
