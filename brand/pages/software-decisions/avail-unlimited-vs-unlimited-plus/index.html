Avail Unlimited vs Unlimited Plus: Lease Customization and Rent Payments

Compare Avail’s landlord plans through a lease-renewal example, payment conditions and a practical acceptance checklist.

Start with the task you repeat

A landlord renewing several leases needs to decide whether the repeated work is collecting signatures or rebuilding the same approved clauses. Those are different purchase requirements. Avail publishes digital leasing in Unlimited; the paid plan adds ways to customize and reuse the materials. Upgrading merely because a lease needs a signature would overlook what the basic plan already contains.

Use a fictional three-property renewal as a demonstration brief. One tenancy keeps its existing terms, another changes a parking arrangement, and the third has a different approved addendum. Ask the demonstrator to show which elements are copied, which remain attached to the old agreement, and how the final signed version is identified. A reusable template is convenient only when the operator can keep the correct version associated with the correct tenancy.

Keep payment timing separate from rent due dates

Avail describes FastPay as a bank-account payment feature that can deliver funds as soon as the next business day after processing. That wording is conditional. Do not build an owner distribution promise around an unconditional next-day arrival. Record the withdrawal, processing and deposit stages separately, and ask how weekends, rejected payments and eligibility affect the example.

The current pricing page also distinguishes who pays bank-transfer charges. Review the tenant-facing payment screen as well as the landlord subscription. A waived transaction charge and an upgraded lease workflow solve different problems; compare both without assuming that every tenant chooses the same payment method. Card-payment terms should be checked independently.

Decide from a completed renewal

Run one sample tenancy from application questions to an executed renewal and a simulated payment history. Save an export or screenshot reference for each acceptance point, without including real tenant data in a sales demonstration. Have the person who will perform renewals—not only the buyer—explain how a mistaken template would be corrected.

This comparison addresses software workflow. It does not establish that a lease clause is appropriate in a particular jurisdiction or that a payment record resolves a dispute. Keep professional review of clauses and the original signed documents in the process.

Purchasing acceptance checklist

  • Identify which properties actually need a reusable customized agreement, rather than assuming every unit requires the upgrade.
  • Ask for the exact subscription unit, renewal terms and tenant payment charges in the dated proposal before comparing annual spending.
  • Test a changed addendum and confirm the signed output retains the property, parties, effective dates and attachment references.
  • Document FastPay eligibility and exceptions; assign someone to reconcile deposits rather than treating a scheduled withdrawal as received rent.

Official sources and scope

Features and commercial terms can change. Confirm current availability, minimum commitments, transaction charges and implementation responsibilities in a written proposal. No subscription totals or performance outcomes are estimated here.
