RentRedi Accounting vs Separate Bookkeeping: Define the Missing Work

Decide whether RentRedi’s accounting records support the required handoff or whether additional bookkeeping work needs a separate scope.

AE Tax Advisors · Published November 20, 2026

Rental software comparisons and selection guides

RentRedi’s official accounting FAQ describes expense capture, property and portfolio profit-and-loss views, and Schedule E summaries connected with its rental activity. That can be useful without establishing that every landlord’s accounting requirement is covered. The decision is to identify the work already supported and any remaining work. This guide does not assert that a landlord must buy another product or that built-in tools replace professional review.

Inventory what is already recorded

List rent received through the platform, rent received elsewhere, deposits, expenses and other relevant transactions. RentRedi’s FAQ says recorded rental income flows into the same financial views used for reports. Ask how external transactions are added and checked. A complete inventory helps distinguish a missing-data problem from a need for another accounting service.

Review categorization and supporting documents

The FAQ describes receipt extraction and the ability to change amounts, categories and property assignments. Set a process for reviewing suggestions and retaining source evidence. Include shared expenses, corrections and duplicate documents in a sample. Do not treat the existence of a category or automated match as a decision about tax treatment.

Define the output the reviewer needs

RentRedi describes exports of expense details, profit-and-loss statements and Schedule E summaries. It explicitly says a Schedule E summary is not the same as filing a tax return. Ask the preparer which records and supplemental schedules are required for the owner’s circumstances. An export is a handoff document, not proof that a return or accounting review has been completed.

Specify any remaining bookkeeping assignment

If additional work is needed, write it down precisely: reconcile an outside account, resolve an unexplained balance, organize historical records or prepare an agreed report. Ask a provider to quote those tasks rather than replacing the entire system by default. Keep software subscription charges, professional work and the landlord’s retained responsibilities separate in the comparison.

Avoid two systems becoming two incomplete records

For a fictional owner using a separate bookkeeper, trace a rent receipt and an expense from entry through export to the final reviewed records. Decide which system is authoritative for each part of the process and how corrections flow back. Without that agreement, adding another system can create duplicate entries or inconsistent reports rather than solve the underlying problem.

Review the arrangement after a complete cycle

After one reporting period, list unresolved items and the person responsible for each. Confirm that documents, corrections and final reports remain accessible to the owner. RentRedi’s statement that accounting features are included in landlord subscriptions does not establish that every service is free or sufficient for every situation. Judge the documented workflow and the current agreed scope.

Decision checklist

  • All in-platform and external transaction sources
  • Owner review of receipt and category suggestions
  • Required exports and supplemental records
  • Specific tasks for any separate bookkeeper
  • Authoritative system and correction handoff
  • One-period reconciliation and unresolved-item list
