AppFolio Plus vs Max: Read-Only Data or Operational Write Access

Compare AppFolio’s higher tiers around API direction, leasing CRM scope and the controls needed for an integration project.

Start with the action outside AppFolio

A dashboard and an operational integration are different purchases. A dashboard may need to read records; a system that changes records needs a supported write operation and a control process. The current plan page draws that distinction between Plus and Max. It does not guarantee that every operation your organization imagines is supported.

Consider a fictional operator connecting a separate leasing intake system. The first requirement is to report how many inquiries reached a property. The second proposed requirement is to update an operational record after a staff review. Document these separately. Ask the vendor to identify the supported records, permitted actions and plan conditions for each, rather than accepting “API available” as a complete answer.

Treat a write as an operational event

For each proposed update, define the person or system authorized to request it, the required validation and the evidence retained afterward. A duplicate request, an outdated property identifier or a partial failure can require a different response from a successful update. The purchasing team should require a demonstrable recovery procedure before depending on automation.

The leasing CRM and Leasing Signals are additional Max scope, not proof that an integration will improve leasing results. Select a concrete handoff: an inquiry moves to an assigned employee, a follow-up is recorded, and a supervisor reviews the history. Ask which steps are native and which depend on an additional service or configuration.

Evaluate support against your operating model

Dedicated customer success is useful to evaluate through an agreed implementation and escalation plan. Ask who handles configuration questions, who supports the custom integration and what the customer remains responsible for. Avoid treating a named support relationship as a promise to build or operate your internal systems.

A read-only reporting project may not require Max. A supported write workflow or the additional leasing scope may justify evaluating it. Compare the actual quoted plan and services, minimum commitments and migration work. Keep data exports and rollback responsibilities in the acceptance record so a change in vendor or contractor does not leave the team without usable operating records.

Purchasing acceptance checklist

  • Inventory proposed integrations by direction and supported record type; distinguish necessary operations from optional automation ideas.
  • Use fictional data to test duplicate submissions, invalid identifiers and failed requests before allowing an operational write.
  • Ask for a leasing handoff demonstration with assignment history and supervisor visibility rather than an outcome forecast.
  • Assign responsibility for support, credentials, exports and recovery in writing, including tasks retained by your own team.

Official sources and scope

Features and commercial terms can change. Confirm current availability, minimum commitments, transaction charges and implementation responsibilities in a written proposal. No subscription totals or performance outcomes are estimated here.
