Buildium Essential vs Growth: Coordinating Unit Turns

Compare Buildium plans using staff scheduling, unit-turn work and reporting requirements rather than a generic feature count.

Published November 21, 2026 · Sources checked November 21, 2026

Choose around a handoff problem

A manager who can already record a work order may still struggle to coordinate a cleaner, a repair visit and a showing on the same vacant unit. The useful question is whether a higher plan changes that handoff. Essential includes the underlying maintenance workflow. Growth adds coordination capabilities that deserve a demonstration with the actual sequence of responsibilities.

Consider a fictional six-unit turnover week with two employees and an outside painter. One repair cannot begin until a part arrives. Ask the vendor to build the dependencies, show the revised calendar, and identify what the leasing employee sees when the expected completion date changes. Record whether the operator must update several separate records or whether the demonstrated workflow carries the change through.

Measure the output, not the label

Buildium’s maintenance materials describe assigned tasks, work orders, estimates, bills and time-stamped updates. Those documented functions provide a starting point for the test. They do not prove that a particular configuration will match your staffing model. Ask which settings and permissions are required to reproduce the example in the purchased plan.

For reporting, choose a specific recipient and deadline. An owner might need a monthly statement while an operations supervisor needs unfinished turns each morning. Request both outputs with the same sample property data. A report that is available only through a manual workaround should be recorded as such, rather than counted as an automated delivery.

Include implementation in the decision

The current pricing page says new Growth and Premium customers must use customized onboarding. Request that scope and charge in writing; a plan headline alone is not the implementation budget. Identify who imports existing tasks, establishes user permissions and confirms opening accounting records before the team begins daily work.

Growth may be relevant when those coordination and reporting requirements recur. A small team with a satisfactory existing handoff process may instead have a different bottleneck. Select from demonstrated requirements and the full proposal, without treating the larger plan as a promise of faster turns or more accurate books.

Purchasing acceptance checklist

  • Write the turnover sequence, responsible person and completion evidence before the sales meeting so the demonstration uses your real decision criteria.
  • Test a delayed vendor task and check whether the showing team receives an appropriate update without exposing unrelated owner information.
  • Request sample supervisor and owner reports, noting manual steps, scheduling options and permissions needed for each.
  • Confirm onboarding deliverables, import responsibilities and acceptance dates in the same proposal as the subscription.

Official sources and scope

Features and commercial terms can change. Confirm current availability, minimum commitments, transaction charges and implementation responsibilities in a written proposal. No subscription totals or performance outcomes are estimated here.
