Rental Sale Tax Review File: Basis, Depreciation and Planned Exit

Prepare the tax review before agreeing to a sale structure. The records needed to evaluate a disposition extend beyond the purchase price and the expected selling price.

Reconstruct the property history

Gather purchase documents, improvement invoices, depreciation schedules and prior study reports. Separate property, land, furnishings and later additions as recorded by the preparer. Flag missing schedules rather than estimating the history from memory.

Identify suspended amounts and ownership

Ask the preparer for the activity-level loss records and applicable basis schedules. Confirm the legal seller and the tax owner, including any changes over time. Give the reviewer the complete ownership picture if the property sits in a partnership or other entity.

Document the proposed transaction

Bring the draft agreement, proposed allocation, selling costs and expected closing date. State whether you are considering an exchange or another exit structure. Coordinate with qualified counsel and any necessary intermediary before taking steps that may constrain the options.

Compare after-tax cash and timing

Request a written estimate that separates proceeds, debt payoff, transaction costs and taxes. Ask how prior depreciation and state treatment affect the comparison. Update the estimate when terms change rather than relying on an early headline number.

Printable preparation worksheet

Use a private working copy to record the document location, outstanding question and person responsible for completing each item. These blank fields do not submit or store information online. Keep sensitive data in your secure records.

Before you request advice

Mark unresolved items clearly and explain any deadline that may affect the review. Ask for an individualized conclusion and a written work scope before relying on a recommendation. A completed preparation worksheet is evidence organization, not approval of a tax position.
