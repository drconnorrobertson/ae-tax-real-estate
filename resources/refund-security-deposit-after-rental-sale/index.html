How Do You Track a Refundable Security Deposit When a Rental Is Sold?

A deposit intended to be returned generally is not rental income at receipt. Review the lease and closing agreement to determine which party holds the deposit and owes any refund after closing.

Worked example

Assume the closing ledger transfers a $1,200 refundable deposit to the buyer. The new owner later refunds $950 and retains $250 for a documented lease violation. If the records support those facts, the buyer’s deposit liability clears through the refund and the retained amount. Review the $250 for rental income when retained. Keep any separate advance rent or rent proration out of this deposit reconciliation.

Records to prepare

  1. List the amount held for each tenant and compare it with the lease and any notices required for the transfer.
  2. Match the transferred funds or closing credit to the liability assumed under the purchase agreement.
  3. Reconcile later refunds and amounts retained against the tenant ledger, bank records, and any damage invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the security deposit automatically become rental income at closing?

A deposit intended to be returned generally is not rental income at receipt. Review the lease and closing agreement to determine which party holds the deposit and owes any refund after closing.

What if the tenant agreed to apply the deposit to last month’s rent?

An amount designated to pay the final month’s rent is advance rent, generally included in rental income when received. Check the lease and payment history before treating it as a refundable liability.

What if part of a refundable deposit is later retained?

The landlord generally includes the retained amount in rental income for the year it is kept because the tenant did not meet the lease terms. Document who retained the amount and why.
